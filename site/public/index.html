Open apps, move windows, and type with your voice

A free launcher for your Mac. Press ⌃⌥Space to open an app or move a window. Hold one key and talk to type in any app, with speech to text that runs on your Mac.

    Other launchers keep adding sign-ins, add-on stores, and AI that runs online. Ciel does three things, all on your Mac.

    Fast at the few things it does

    Finds what you meant

    Prefixes, abbreviations like vsc, typos like safrai, accents, and words in any order, in a few milliseconds. Exact matches always win over history.

    • safraiSafari
    • vscVisual Studio Code
    • calclatorCalculator
    • set sysSystem Settings

    Puts windows where you want them

    Eighteen commands for halves, quarters, three fourths, center, almost maximize, restore, minimize, and moving between displays. Bind any of them to a global shortcut.

    Types what you say

    Hold one key, talk, and let go. Ciel pastes the words into the app you were using.

    Nothing you did not ask for

    No store, plugins, account, or cloud. One process with no idle timer, and the app list refreshes only when apps change.

    Remembers where you put it

    Drag the launcher and it snaps to guides at each quarter of the screen. Every display keeps its own spot.

    Dictation that stays on your Mac

    Notes
    Right ⌘
    1. Right ⌘

      Hold one key and talk

      Set Right ⌘, Right ⌥, or fn as the dictation shortcut. Hold it while you speak, or tap it to start and tap again to stop.

    2. Let go and it pastes

      A small pill shows the words as you say them. Ciel transcribes while you talk, so the text pastes into the front app soon after you stop.

    3. Turn it on once

      Speech to text is off until you turn it on in Settings and download the speech model. macOS asks for microphone access the first time.

    Three keys between you and any app

    1. ⌃⌥Space

      Open Ciel from anywhere

      Control Option Space works over any app. Record a different shortcut in Settings.

    2. saf

      Type a few letters

      Results appear as you type. Tab switches between all results, apps, and window commands.

    3. Return

      Press Return

      Or hold Command and press 1 through 9 to run one of the first nine results.

    What stays on your Mac

    • Your voice

      The Parakeet Ultra model turns speech into text on your Mac. Audio stays in memory while you talk and is never saved or sent.

    • Your settings and history

      No account, server, or analytics. Ciel keeps your settings, shortcuts, and launch history on your Mac and never sends them.

    • One download you start

      The only network request is the 550 MB speech model download, and it starts only when you choose Download in Settings. Ciel checks its size and SHA-256 before using it.

    Questions

    How do I get Ciel?

    Build it from source for now. Clone the repository and run bash scripts/build.sh and bash scripts/install.sh with a Swift 6 toolchain. Apple's Command Line Tools are enough, and the README has the steps. A signed download comes once Ciel has an Apple Developer ID.

    Is Ciel free?

    Yes. Ciel is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, trial, or paid tier.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It does not run on Intel Macs.

    Why does Ciel ask for Accessibility access?

    macOS needs it before Ciel can move other apps' windows, paste what you dictate, or use a single key as the dictation shortcut. Without it, app search still works and dictated text goes to the clipboard. Choose Allow Access in Ciel Settings, then turn Ciel on in Privacy and Security.

    Can it replace Spotlight?

    It opens apps and moves windows, but it does not search files or the web. To open Ciel with Command Space, turn off the Spotlight shortcut in System Settings, then record it in Ciel Settings.

    Does it work with full screen apps and Spaces?

    Window commands work on regular windows. They do not work on full screen windows or move windows between Spaces.

    How do I uninstall it?

    To delete the speech model, choose Remove next to Speech model in Settings. Then quit Ciel from its menu bar icon and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To remove its saved settings too, run defaults delete app.mauriciopolvora.ciel in Terminal.
